Watching Netflix together
  1. Open Netflix in another window, on the same title, both of you.
  2. Come back here and press Pop out faces. Your two faces lift off the page into a small window that stays on top of everything else.
  3. Drag that little window wherever you want it, then click back onto Netflix.
  4. Press Count us in. You will both see 3, 2, 1, then the word Play. Press play on Netflix the moment it lands.

Netflix cannot play inside this app. Its copy protection blocks any other site from showing it, so the film has to run in its own window and only the countdown keeps you together. The same is true of Prime, Disney+ and the rest.
